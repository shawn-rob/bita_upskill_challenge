# Archiving and Compressing #

Archiving is different than compressing, but they do come together sometimes.
With archiving, its merely zipping it all together. 
With compression, it's making a file smaller. Often with archiving, compression happens, 
but it is not necessary. It has to be instructed to compress.

---
In Windows, **zip** is the usual extenstion, but in Linux it's **tar or gz**. Tar archives multiple files with the option to compress and gzip compresses a single file.
